# Vendor survey summary

Run by procurement in January 2026. 212 of about 900 active vendors responded.

## Who responded

| Vendor size | Share |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 20 employees | 41% |
| 20 to 100 employees | 33% |
| More than 100 employees | 26% |

## How would you prefer to submit bills?

| Channel | Share |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp | 58% |
| Email | 27% |
| Web portal | 9% |
| Paper at the property | 6% |

## Biggest problem with getting paid

| Problem | Share |
|---|---|
| Do not know where my bill is | 71% |
| Queries come late | 48% |
| Same bill delivered to several places | 33% |
| Payment amount differs from bill without explanation | 19% |

Respondents could pick more than one problem.

## Other findings

- Vendors call the property or head office 3 times per bill on average to ask for status
- 64% said they would use a status update sent on WhatsApp
- 12% said they already use a portal for another large client and find it hard to use
