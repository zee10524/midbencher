# Vendor payment policy (extract)

## Payment terms

- MSME vendors registered as micro or small: payment within 45 days of accepting the goods or services, as required by law
- All other vendors: payment within 60 days of a valid invoice, unless the work order says otherwise
- Payment runs happen every Tuesday and Friday

## Valid invoice

A bill counts as valid when all of these are true:

- It is received at the property facility desk with a tracker number
- It passes the property and head office checklists
- The service entry or GRN is posted in SAP

## Records

- Original bills and supporting documents are filed at head office
- Records are kept for the period required under GST law
- Scans may be used for reference

## MSME status

- Vendors must share their Udyam registration at onboarding
- Procurement updates the vendor master when a vendor shares a new or changed registration
