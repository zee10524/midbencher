# Interview: Property Head

- **Role:** Property Head, office tower
- **Location:** Gurugram
- **Date:** 5 March 2026
- **Interviewer:** Riklr FDE, on site

---

*The interview took place in the property head's office on the ground floor of the tower, next to the building management room. A screen on the wall showed lift and DG status. He came in ten minutes late from a tenant meeting.*

**Property Head:** Apologies. One of the tenants on the ninth floor says the AC is not cooling. In March. I told them it is a setpoint issue, but they want the engineer to come and stand there. So the engineer is standing there. Please, sit. Water? Tea?

**Interviewer:** Water is fine. Thanks for squeezing this in. How long have you been running this tower?

**Property Head:** Four years here. Before that two residential societies in Sonipat. Office is easier in some ways. Residents call you at eleven at night about parking. Tenants only call during office hours. *(laughs)* But tenants have lawyers.

**Interviewer:** What is your part in the bill process?

**Property Head:** I sign the packet. My signature says the work was done and the bill is correct. I do not check every line. My commercial executive does that. I look at the summary and anything unusual.

**Interviewer:** What counts as unusual?

**Property Head:** Amount higher than last month without a reason. A new vendor I have not heard of. Diesel quantity that looks high for the hours the DG ran. Once a pest control bill came for a floor that was vacant. I caught that one. Small things, but that is why the signature is there.

**Interviewer:** How quickly do bills leave your property?

**Property Head:** Fast. Once my team has it, it is out in a day or two. We are not the problem. I have seen bills sit at head office for weeks.

**Interviewer:** How do you know how long they sit at head office?

**Property Head:** Vendors tell me. They come to me when they are not paid. I call central accounts, they say it is in query or it has not reached them. Then I check with my team and they say it went on Thursday.

**Interviewer:** When did that last happen?

**Property Head:** Two weeks back. The DG AMC vendor. Small firm, owner and four technicians. He came here himself, stood outside my door. He said his October and November bills were not paid. I called central accounts. They said October was in query because the service report was not signed. November they could not find. I called my commercial executive. She said both went in the courier. So who lost it? Nobody knows. The vendor said, sir, I will stop the monthly service. I said please do not, we have a tenant audit in two weeks. I had to request him. In my own building.

**Interviewer:** How did it get resolved?

**Property Head:** My executive traced the November packet. The tracker number on the cover was written wrong, so head office had logged it under another vendor. Then they found it. The October one, I signed the service report again. I think both are paid now. I have not heard from him, so I assume.

**Interviewer:** How often do you sign?

**Property Head:** I try every few days. Some weeks I am in back-to-back meetings with the residents' association or the tenants and the folder waits. But it does not wait long.

*His phone rang. He looked at it and stood up.*

**Property Head:** Sorry, I have to take this. Tenant's admin head.

*He stepped out for about four minutes and came back.*

**Property Head:** The AC. Now they want a written report. Fine. Where were we?

**Interviewer:** How bills move from here to head office.

**Property Head:** Courier, once a week. It has always been like that. Thursday pickup.

**Interviewer:** Has anyone considered more frequent pickups?

**Property Head:** It came up once. Somebody said cost. Also, who will make the packets twice a week? My executive is already full. I did not push it. Honestly, a few days here and there is not the issue. The issue is what happens after.

**Interviewer:** What frustrates you about the process?

**Property Head:** The queries. My team checks everything. Then head office checks everything again and asks us the same questions. If they do not trust our check, why do we do it? Either they check or we check.

**Interviewer:** Can you give an example of a query you felt was repeated?

**Property Head:** Attendance. My executive spends days on the security attendance. Biometric, gate register, the agency's sheet, all matched. Department head signs. I sign. Then head office writes back, please confirm attendance for guard number seven on the fourteenth. We already confirmed. That is what my signature means. It is not a personal thing. It just feels like nobody trusts the property.

**Interviewer:** Have you raised that?

**Property Head:** In every review. The answer is audit. Fine. But then let us not do it twice.

**Interviewer:** What do other property heads say?

**Property Head:** Same thing. We have a WhatsApp group, all the property heads. Half the messages are about vendors, half are about head office. Somebody posts a query email and everybody puts the laughing emoji. It is not very professional, I know. But it is how people feel.

**Interviewer:** Do you see the bill status anywhere?

**Property Head:** The register. My facility desk updates it when the bill comes in and when it goes out. After that, nothing. We do not get updates from head office into the register.

**Interviewer:** Could I look at the register?

**Property Head:** Of course. Let me call the desk. *(on intercom)* Please send me the bill register file on email, and send the visitor a copy also. *(to interviewer)* She will send it. It is an Excel. In and out dates, vendor, amount, tracker number. Remarks column, which people use for everything. You will see.

**Interviewer:** Who else gets the register?

**Property Head:** Head office, every week. I do not know who reads it. Nobody has ever called me about it.

**Interviewer:** What have you tried yourself to speed things up?

**Property Head:** I told the facility desk to give the vendor a photocopy of the stamped first page, so at least he has proof it was received here. That stopped some arguments. And I forward vendor complaints to the AP lead on WhatsApp directly, with the tracker number. She replies, mostly. That is about all I can do from here. The rest is not in my hands.

**Interviewer:** How do vendors treat you because of the delays?

**Property Head:** Some of them have worked here longer than me. They are polite, but they are tired. When payment is late, the service drops a little. Not officially. The housekeeping becomes slower, the AMC visit becomes shorter. I cannot prove it. But I see it. And then the tenant complains to me, and I cannot tell the tenant that head office has not paid.

**Interviewer:** If you could change one thing?

**Property Head:** One check, done once, by whoever is best placed. And give the vendor a way to see where his bill is, so he stops calling me.
