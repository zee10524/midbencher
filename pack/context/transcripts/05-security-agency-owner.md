# Interview: Vendor, security agency owner

- **Role:** Owner, security agency supplying guards to 6 properties
- **Location:** Faridabad, by phone
- **Date:** 6 March 2026
- **Interviewer:** Riklr FDE

---

*The call started ten minutes late. The agency owner was driving back from a site and asked to continue on speaker. There was traffic noise for the first few minutes.*

**Agency Owner:** Hello? Can you hear me? I am on the highway, one minute, let me move to the left lane. Okay. Now tell me.

**Interviewer:** I can hear you. Thank you for speaking with us. The CFO said you might be direct with us. That is what we want.

**Agency Owner:** *(laughs)* He said that? Good. I respect him. He picks up my call. Not everybody does. Okay, ask.

**Interviewer:** How long have you worked with the company?

**Agency Owner:** Seven years. We supply around ninety guards across six of their properties. We are a small company. Micro, registered under MSME.

**Interviewer:** Does the company have your MSME certificate?

**Agency Owner:** I registered three years ago. I sent it to procurement by email. Nobody confirmed. I do not know if it is in their system.

**Interviewer:** Did you follow up?

**Agency Owner:** Twice. Once on email, once I told the commercial executive at one property to tell procurement. She said she will. After that, nothing. My CA told me the law says forty five days for MSME. I laughed. Forty five days. Okay.

**Interviewer:** Walk me through how you get paid.

**Agency Owner:** First of the month, the property gives attendance. Sometimes on the third, sometimes the eighth, depends on the supervisor. Then my accountant makes the bill. I print it, attach attendance, PF challan, ESIC challan, and my staff go to the facility desk at each property. Six properties, six trips.

**Interviewer:** Who does the trips?

**Agency Owner:** My office boy, on his bike. Faridabad to Gurugram, then Gurugram to the other side. Two days for six properties if traffic is okay. Petrol is my cost. And if the facility desk person is on lunch, he waits. Once he waited two hours. I told him, next time come back. He said, sir, then I have to come again tomorrow.

**Interviewer:** And then?

**Agency Owner:** Then nothing. I do not know anything. Did it reach head office? Is there a query? Nobody tells me until I call. I call the property, they say it has gone. I call head office, they say it has not come or it is in query. Usually I call three, four times for each bill.

**Interviewer:** Who do you call at head office?

**Agency Owner:** There is one boy in accounts. Good boy. He picks up. I do not know if it is his property or not, but he checks for me. If he leaves the company I am finished. *(laughs)* I am joking. Little bit joking.

**Interviewer:** How do you keep track of all the bills?

**Agency Owner:** My accountant has one Excel. Six properties, twelve months, one row per bill. When it went, who she called, what they said, when payment came. She colours it. Green paid, yellow waiting, red query. In February the sheet was mostly yellow and red. I print it every week and keep it in my diary. When I go for any meeting with the company I take it.

**Interviewer:** How long until you are paid?

**Agency Owner:** Five weeks if everything is fine. Sometimes two months. But I pay my guards on the seventh. Every month. If I do not, they leave and the property complains to me. So I take a loan from the bank to pay salaries. The interest is my cost.

**Interviewer:** When was the longest wait recently?

**Agency Owner:** Diwali time. October bill. Guards also want their bonus at Diwali, so that month I needed more money, not less. They held it because the work order was being renewed. I did not even know it was being renewed. I found out when I called. I paid salaries and bonus from my overdraft. Paid in December. Two months. I remember because my wife asked why I was not sleeping.

**Interviewer:** What causes the long ones?

**Agency Owner:** Queries. Last month they said IGST is wrong. Our office is in Faridabad, the property is in Gurugram, both Haryana. My new accountant put IGST. My mistake, fine. But I came to know after three weeks. Then I have to make a credit note and a new bill, print, go again.

**Interviewer:** How did you find out about it?

**Agency Owner:** I called. That is how I find out everything. The boy in accounts said, sir, there is a query since two weeks, did you not get the email? The email went to the old accountant's ID. She left in December. My fault also, I did not update. But if somebody had sent one WhatsApp, I would have fixed it the same evening.

**Interviewer:** Any queries that were not your mistake?

**Agency Owner:** Many. Work order finished, so they cannot book. How is that my problem? I supplied the guards they asked for. One time they held two months because the work order was being renewed.

*He paused. A second phone rang in the background.*

**Agency Owner:** One second, sorry. My supervisor. *(to the other phone)* Haan, bolo. Kaun nahi aaya? Okay, send the spare guard from the other site, I will adjust. Haan. *(back to the interviewer)* Sorry. One guard did not come for the night shift at a mall. Every day something.

**Interviewer:** No problem. Those relievers, do they get counted correctly in your bills?

**Agency Owner:** This is another fight. If a guard is absent and I send a reliever, the reliever signs the gate register. He is not in the biometric because he is not registered at that site. Then the property says, biometric says only so many days. I say, check the register. They say, okay, we will check. That check takes one week. Sometimes they cut the days. I accept it because what can I do. I want the bill to move.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever stopped supply because of late payment?

**Agency Owner:** Never. I cannot. Guards are not like diesel. If I pull the guards, the residents will be unsafe and I will lose the contract forever. They know that. Everybody knows that. So I take the loan.

**Interviewer:** How would you prefer to submit bills?

**Agency Owner:** WhatsApp. Everyone in my office has WhatsApp. Email also fine. I do not want another website with another password. I already have four portals for other clients. One of them I never managed to log in.

**Interviewer:** What happened with that one?

**Agency Owner:** They sent a link, I made a password, then it said my GST number is already registered by somebody. Probably my old accountant tried. I called their helpline. They gave me a ticket number. That was last year. I still send that client paper. *(laughs)* So please, no portal.

**Interviewer:** If you could see the status of each bill, what would you want to know?

**Agency Owner:** Simple. Received. Checked at property. With head office. Query, and what the query is. Paid, and the date. That is all.

**Interviewer:** Anything else?

**Agency Owner:** Tell me about the query the same day. Not after three weeks. If it is my mistake, I fix it the same day.

**Interviewer:** If it is not your mistake?

**Agency Owner:** Then also tell me. At least I know whom to chase. Right now I chase everybody, and everybody says it is somebody else. The property says head office. Head office says property. Procurement does not pick up. I am not angry with any one person. I am angry with the whole thing.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. That is very helpful.

**Agency Owner:** One more thing. Tell the CFO, I am not complaining for fun. Seven years I have not left. But my margins are small. Every month I wait, I pay interest on his bill. Okay? Okay. I have reached the office. Thank you, thank you.
