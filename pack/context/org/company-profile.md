# Company profile

## The company

- NCR based real estate developer with residential, commercial and retail projects across Haryana, Delhi and western Uttar Pradesh
- Builds and sells projects, then runs many of them through its Property Management division
- Group revenue and project details are not relevant to this case

## Property Management division

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Properties managed | 38 (28 residential societies, 8 office towers, 2 retail malls) |
| Cities | Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat, Panchkula, New Delhi, Noida, Ghaziabad |
| Active vendors | About 900 |
| Vendor bills per year | About 11,000 |
| Largest spend categories | Security manpower, housekeeping manpower, diesel |

## Teams in the bill process

| Team | Where | People | Role in a bill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facility desk | Each property | 1 to 2 per property | Receives the paper bill, stamps it, logs it in the property register |
| Commercial executive | Each property | 1 per property, some cover 2 | Checks the bill against the work order and support documents. Creates the service entry or GRN in SAP. |
| Department heads | Each property | Security, housekeeping, engineering | Sign attendance and service reports |
| Property head | Each property | 1 | Signs off the bill packet |
| Central accounts payable | Head office, Gurugram | 5 verifiers and 1 lead | Rechecks the packet, raises queries, posts the invoice in SAP |
| Treasury | Head office | 2 | Approves and releases payments |
| Procurement | Head office | 4 | Creates and amends work orders and purchase orders |
| IT | Head office | 6, plus vendors | Runs SAP and internal tools |

## Systems

| System | Used for |
|---|---|
| SAP | Work orders, purchase orders, service entries, GRNs, invoice posting, payments |
| Excel register | One per property. Logs each bill on arrival with a tracker number. Shared to head office weekly. |
| Email | Queries between central AP, properties and vendors |
| Biometric attendance | Security and housekeeping punches at the gate on most properties |
| Paper gate register | Manual in and out record at the gate, kept alongside biometric |
| Shared drive | Scans of some bill packets. Usage varies by property. |
| WhatsApp | Day-to-day coordination between properties, vendors and head office. Not an official record. |

## The bill journey as documented

1. Vendor prints the bill with supporting documents and hands it at the property facility desk
2. Facility desk stamps it, assigns a tracker number and logs it in the register
3. Commercial executive checks it and creates the service entry or GRN in SAP
4. Property head signs the packet
5. Packets go to head office by courier
6. Central AP rechecks the packet and either raises a query or posts the invoice
7. Treasury pays in the next payment run
